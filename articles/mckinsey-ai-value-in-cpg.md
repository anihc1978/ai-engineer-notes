# Fortune or Fiction? The Real Value of a Digital and AI Transformation in CPG

**Source:** McKinsey & Company  
**URL:** https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/consumer-packaged-goods/our-insights/fortune-or-fiction-the-real-value-of-a-digital-and-ai-transformation-in-cpg  
**Date:** October 3, 2024  
**Authors:** Jessica Moulton, Rob Cain, Roger Roberts, Hannah Mayer, Spurthi Gummadala

---

## Summary

McKinsey analysis quantifying the EBITDA impact of digital and AI transformation across the CPG value chain. Based on research into 140+ use cases across seven value streams.

---

## Key Stats

- **71%** of CPG leaders adopted AI in at least one business function in 2024 (up from 42% in 2023)
- - **56%** were regularly using gen AI
  - - Gen AI use cases could increase the economic impact of traditional AI by **15–40%**
    - - That unlocks an additional **$160B–$270B annually** in profit (EBITDA) for CPG companies globally
      - - Traditional AI's potential impact is **2.5–7.0x higher** than gen AI alone

        | Subsector | EBITDA Impact | Margin Increase |
        |---|---|---|

        ## The Seven Value Streams

        1. **Consumer Insights & Demand Shaping** – largest value driver for most sectors
        2. 2. **Customer & Channel Management** – largest for food & beverage; trade promotion optimization is key
           3. 3. **Direct-to-Consumer (DTC)** – largest for beauty brands
              4. 4. Product & Innovation
                 5. 5. Supply Chain & Operations
                    6. 6. Enterprise & Support Functions
                       7. 7. Ecosystem & Partnerships
                         
                          8. ---
                         
                          9. ## Key Takeaways
                         
                          10. - Highest-impact areas vary by CPG subsector
                              - - Greatest value concentrated in **consumer insights/demand shaping** and **customer/channel management**
                                - - Beauty exception: **DTC value stream** takes center stage
                                  - - Data exchange with ecosystem partners (retailers, manufacturers, logistics, media) unlocks the next level of value
                                    - - CPG companies have historically lacked first-party data at scale — investing in zero-party/first-party data acquisition is critical
                                      - - Full transformation requires all-hands-on-deck across commercial, operational, and support domains — not a piecemeal approach

                                        ## Use Case Examples

                                        - **Beverage company:** Used gen AI for product development prompts/concepts → reduced time-to-market by **60%**
                                        - - **Personal care player:** AI improved forecast accuracy by **13%**, decreased product shortages by **40%**, decreased inventory by **35%**
                                          - - **Beauty brand:** AI-powered at-home lipstick system that reads color from photos and formulates custom lipstick
                                           
                                            - ---

                                            ## Notes

                                            - Traditional AI >> gen AI in total value potential (gen AI is "the cherry on top")
                                            - - Companies should invest in a broad spectrum of AI capabilities, not just gen AI
                                              - - CPGs lag other industries (advanced industries, TMT) in gen AI adoption rates
                                                - - "Rewired" companies take a comprehensive digital road map approach: talent, operating model, tech tools, data products
